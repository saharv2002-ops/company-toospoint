\begin{clausebox}

This \textbf{Consulting Agreement} (the ``Agreement'') is entered into between

\end{clausebox}

\vspace{10pt}

%% ── Section 1 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Scope of Services}

Consultant will provide strategic growth, business-development, and
partnership-execution support to the Company across the following core focus
areas:

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Strategic LSP Partnerships}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Identification and prioritization of high-value LSP partnership opportunities aligned with the Company's service mix and growth strategy
  \item Warm introductions to LSP decision-makers where appropriate, leveraging Consultant's existing industry relationships
  \item Development of customized partnership positioning, capability presentations, and vendor applications
  \item Support for contract and rate negotiations, and build-out of long-term white-label and overflow partnerships
  \item Initial priority on positioning the Company as a strategic vendor with \textbf{Propio} across interpreting, translation, localization, and adjacent service lines
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Direct Client Growth \& Market Expansion}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Identification of high-value prospects and design of targeted outreach campaigns across healthcare, government, insurance, financial services, legal, enterprise, and BPO verticals
  \item Development of account strategies, sales messaging, and discovery-meeting support
  \item Proposal development, pricing strategy, and competitive positioning advisory
  \item Build-out and maintenance of a long-term sales pipeline
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}RFP Strategy \& Management}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Identification of relevant public sector and private enterprise RFP opportunities and evaluation of bid/no-bid decisions
  \item Creation of proposal timelines and coordination of internal deliverables
  \item Drafting of technical and operational responses, staffing and implementation plans, and pricing review
  \item Final quality review before submission
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Strategic Business Development Support}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Strategic advisory to leadership on market positioning, competitive analysis, and go-to-market strategy
  \item Partnership strategy, sales process optimization, growth planning
  \item Operational readiness for enterprise clients
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Vendor \& Enterprise Readiness}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Preparation of capability statements, vendor questionnaires, and operational documentation
  \item Quality assurance positioning and recruitment strategy alignment
  \item Service delivery readiness and enterprise presentation materials
\end{itemize}

\begin{clausebox}
\sffamily\footnotesize\color{TPGray}
\textbf{Advisory Capacity.} All recommendations made by Consultant are advisory
in nature. Final decision-making authority on all business, commercial, and
operational matters rests with the Company.
\end{clausebox}

%% ── Section 2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Compensation}

Company agrees to pay Consultant a \textbf{monthly retainer of TBD USD}
for services rendered under this Agreement. Consultant shall submit a monthly
invoice on or near the first business day of each month. Payment is due
\textbf{Net~15} from the invoice date, payable via \textbf{wire transfer,
Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal}.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item The monthly retainer is a flat fee and is not contingent on a specific deliverable count beyond the milestones expressly identified herein
  \item No commission, revenue share, or success fee is payable under this Agreement; compensation is strictly the monthly retainer
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 3 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Time Commitment}

Consultant shall make available approximately \textbf{TBD hours per calendar
week} in support of the services described herein. Hours may be scheduled
flexibly to accommodate the needs of the Company and the availability of
Consultant.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Hours not used in a given week do not roll forward and do not accrue as a credit
  \item Material and sustained increases beyond the weekly commitment shall be discussed and agreed in writing, and may result in an adjusted retainer
  \item Consultant retains discretion over the methods, sequence, and timing of work, provided service obligations are met
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 4 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Term and Termination}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item This Agreement will begin on \textbf{TBD} (the ``Effective Date'') and continue on a \textbf{month-to-month} basis until terminated.
  \item Either party may terminate this Agreement with \textbf{30~days written notice}.
  \item Upon termination, Company shall pay Consultant the prorated portion of the monthly retainer for services rendered through the effective termination date.
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 5 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Independent Contractor}

Consultant is an \textbf{independent contractor} and not an employee, agent, or
partner of the Company. Consultant retains sole responsibility for all taxes,
benefits, insurance, and obligations arising from Consultant's business
operations. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an
employment, partnership, joint-venture, or agency relationship. Consultant has
no authority to bind the Company or to enter into any agreement on the
Company's behalf without prior written authorization.

%% ── Section 6 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Confidentiality}

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of each other's proprietary
information, trade secrets, client and prospect lists, partner relationships,
pricing data, financials, and business strategies disclosed in the course of
this engagement. This obligation shall:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of \textbf{three~(3)~years}
  \item Not apply to information that is publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
  \item Not restrict either party from disclosures required by law or court order, provided that prompt written notice is given to the other party where lawful
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 7 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Ownership of Work Product}

All deliverables, sales materials, RFP responses, prospect lists, and work
product specifically prepared by Consultant for Company under this Agreement
shall, upon full payment of the applicable monthly retainer, be owned by
Company. Consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing methodologies,
templates, frameworks, outreach playbooks, and general know-how, and nothing
in this Agreement shall be construed as a transfer of such pre-existing
intellectual property. Consultant is granted a perpetual right to reference
the engagement generally for portfolio and credentialing purposes, subject to
the confidentiality obligations herein.

%% ── Section 8 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Non-Solicitation}

During the term of this Agreement and for \textbf{twelve (12)~months}
thereafter, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee,
contractor, or partner introduced by the other party in connection with this
engagement, without the other party's prior written consent. General public
job postings shall not be considered solicitation.

%% ── Section 9 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Limitation of Liability}

Except for breaches of confidentiality or willful misconduct, neither party
shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, special,
or punitive damages arising out of or related to this Agreement. Consultant's
aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid
by Company to Consultant during the three (3)~months immediately preceding the
event giving rise to the claim.

%% ── Section 10 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Governing Law}

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with applicable
laws as agreed upon by both parties. Any disputes arising under this Agreement
that cannot be resolved through good-faith negotiation shall be submitted to
binding arbitration before commencing litigation. This Agreement constitutes
the entire understanding between the parties with respect to its subject
matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. It may be amended
only by a written instrument signed by both parties, and may be executed in
counterparts, including by electronic signature.
