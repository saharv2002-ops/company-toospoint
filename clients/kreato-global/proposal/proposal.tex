%% ── Section 1 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Strategic LSP Partnerships}

Leverage ToosPoint's industry relationships to introduce Kreato Global to
established Language Service Providers looking for qualified interpreting and
language partners.

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Scope}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Identify and prioritize high-value LSP partnership opportunities
  \item Warm introductions to decision makers where appropriate
  \item Develop customized partnership positioning
  \item Assist with capability presentations
  \item Prepare vendor applications
  \item Support contract and rate negotiations
  \item Build long-term white label and overflow partnerships
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Initial Focus}

One of the first priorities will be positioning Kreato Global as a strategic
vendor with \textbf{Propio} across multiple service lines, including
interpreting, translation, localization, and any additional opportunities
that align with Propio's vendor ecosystem. This includes guiding the vendor
onboarding process, facilitating introductions where appropriate, and helping
position Kreato Global as a scalable, high-quality partner.

Additional LSP partnership opportunities will also be identified based on
market fit and growth potential.

%% ── Term ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Term, Termination \& Confidentiality}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Engagement runs on a \textbf{month-to-month} basis from the effective date, terminable by either party with \textbf{30 days written notice}
  \item Upon termination, Kreato Global pays Consultant the prorated portion of the monthly retainer through the effective termination date
  \item Both parties hold each other's confidential information (prospect lists, partner relationships, pricing, financials, strategy) in strict confidence, surviving termination for \textbf{three (3)~years}
  \item Work product specifically prepared for Kreato Global --- decks, RFP responses, prospect lists, and related materials --- belongs to Kreato Global upon payment, while Consultant retains pre-existing methodologies, templates, and frameworks
\end{itemize}
