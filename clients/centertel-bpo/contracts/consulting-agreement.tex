%% ── Preamble ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{clausebox}
\sffamily\small
This \textbf{Consulting Agreement} (the ``Agreement'') is entered into between
\textbf{ToosPoint Consulting} (``Consultant'') and \textbf{CenterTel BPO}
(``Company''). The parties agree to the terms and conditions set forth herein.
\end{clausebox}

\vspace{10pt}

%% ── Section 1 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Scope of Services}

Consultant will provide ongoing advisory and execution support to the Company
across the following core focus areas:

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Strategic Operational Guidance}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Review and refinement of day-to-day operational workflows, escalation paths, and quality assurance practices
  \item Recommendations on call-center operations, scheduling, capacity planning, and service-level performance
  \item Process documentation, standard operating procedures, and continuous-improvement initiatives
  \item Tooling and technology assessment (telephony, scheduling, CRM, interpretation platforms, reporting)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Employee Coaching \& Training}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item One-on-one coaching for managers, team leads, and individual contributors
  \item Design and delivery of training sessions on customer service, quality, professionalism, and operational excellence
  \item Performance feedback frameworks, coaching cadences, and career-development pathways
  \item Conflict resolution, accountability practices, and team-culture development
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Interpreter Development}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Recruitment, onboarding, and credentialing guidance for interpreter staff and contractors
  \item Quality monitoring, calibration, and skill-development programs across language pairs and specialty domains (medical, legal, social-services)
  \item Continuing-education frameworks, mentorship structures, and language-access compliance posture
  \item Retention strategy, scheduling optimization, and interpreter-experience improvements
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}Business Growth Support}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Go-to-market guidance, pricing review, and service-line expansion strategy
  \item Client retention, account expansion, and customer-success practices
  \item Pipeline and partnership development, including support for proposals, RFPs, and new business pursuits as mutually agreed
  \item Market positioning, competitive intelligence, and brand-presence recommendations
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Strategic Advisory Support}

In furtherance of the Scope of Services, Consultant may participate in
\textbf{leadership meetings, employee meetings, client discussions, vendor
meetings,} and other business activities reasonably related to the services
contemplated under this Agreement. Such participation may take place in person,
by telephone, or by video conference, at times mutually agreed by the parties.

Consultant may also provide recommendations regarding:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Organizational structure and reporting relationships
  \item Staffing levels, role design, and workforce planning
  \item Technology selection, integration, and operational tooling
  \item Internal policies, procedures, and governance practices
  \item Service delivery models and customer-experience standards
  \item Performance management, KPIs, and accountability frameworks
  \item Short- and long-term growth strategy, including new markets, products, and partnerships
\end{itemize}

\begin{clausebox}
\sffamily\footnotesize\color{TPGray}
\textbf{Advisory Capacity.} All recommendations made by Consultant are advisory
in nature. Final decision-making authority on all business, personnel, and
operational matters rests with the Company.
\end{clausebox}

%% ── Section 3 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Compensation}

Company agrees to pay Consultant a \textbf{monthly retainer of \$5{,}000 USD}
for services rendered under this Agreement. Consultant shall submit a monthly
invoice on or near the first business day of each month. Payment is due
\textbf{Net~15} from the invoice date, payable via \textbf{wire transfer,
Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal}.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item The monthly retainer is a flat fee and is not contingent on a specific deliverable count
  \item No commission, revenue share, or success fee is payable under this Agreement; compensation is strictly the monthly retainer
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 4 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Time Commitment}

Consultant shall make available a \textbf{minimum of twenty (20)~hours per
calendar week} in support of the services described herein. Hours may be
scheduled flexibly to accommodate the needs of the Company and the availability
of Consultant.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Hours not used in a given week do not roll forward and do not accrue as a credit
  \item Material and sustained increases beyond the weekly commitment shall be discussed and agreed in writing, and may result in an adjusted retainer
  \item Consultant retains discretion over the methods, sequence, and timing of work, provided service obligations are met
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 5 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Term and Termination}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item This Agreement will begin on \textbf{July~20, 2026} (the ``Effective Date'') and continue on a \textbf{month-to-month} basis until terminated.
  \item Either party may terminate this Agreement with \textbf{30~days written notice}.
  \item Upon termination, Company shall pay Consultant the prorated portion of the monthly retainer for services rendered through the effective termination date.
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 6 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Independent Contractor}

Consultant is an \textbf{independent contractor} and not an employee, agent, or
partner of the Company. Consultant retains sole responsibility for all taxes,
benefits, insurance, and obligations arising from Consultant's business
operations. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an
employment, partnership, joint-venture, or agency relationship. Consultant has
no authority to bind the Company or to enter into any agreement on the
Company's behalf without prior written authorization.

%% ── Section 7 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Confidentiality}

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of each other's proprietary
information, trade secrets, client lists, interpreter rosters, pricing data,
financials, and business strategies disclosed in the course of this engagement.
This obligation shall:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of \textbf{three~(3)~years}
  \item Not apply to information that is publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
  \item Not restrict either party from disclosures required by law or court order, provided that prompt written notice is given to the other party where lawful
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 8 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Ownership of Work Product}

All deliverables, reports, training materials, and work product specifically
prepared by Consultant for Company under this Agreement shall, upon full
payment of the applicable monthly retainer, be owned by Company. Consultant
retains ownership of its pre-existing methodologies, templates, frameworks,
coaching curricula, and general know-how, and nothing in this Agreement shall
be construed as a transfer of such pre-existing intellectual property.
Consultant is granted a perpetual right to reference the engagement
generally for portfolio and credentialing purposes, subject to the
confidentiality obligations herein.

%% ── Section 9 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Non-Solicitation}

During the term of this Agreement and for \textbf{twelve (12)~months}
thereafter, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee,
contractor, or interpreter introduced by the other party in connection with
this engagement, without the other party's prior written consent. General
public job postings shall not be considered solicitation.

%% ── Section 10 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Limitation of Liability}

Except for breaches of confidentiality or willful misconduct, neither party
shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, consequential, special,
or punitive damages arising out of or related to this Agreement. Consultant's
aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid
by Company to Consultant during the three (3)~months immediately preceding the
event giving rise to the claim.

%% ── Section 11 ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Governing Law}

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with applicable
laws as agreed upon by both parties. Any disputes arising under this Agreement
that cannot be resolved through good-faith negotiation shall be submitted to
binding arbitration before commencing litigation. This Agreement constitutes
the entire understanding between the parties with respect to its subject
matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements. It may be amended
only by a written instrument signed by both parties, and may be executed in
counterparts, including by electronic signature.
