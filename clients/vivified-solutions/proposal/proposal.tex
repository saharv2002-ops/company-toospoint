%% ── Section 2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Why ToosPoint}

Vivified Solutions is at the stage where the next dollar of growth comes from
disciplined outbound, strong written materials, and a few well-run RFPs --- not
from spreading attention across every possible channel. ToosPoint brings:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Direct industry relationships} across the LSP ecosystem that
        Sahar can activate in the first weeks to open partnership conversations
        that would otherwise take months of cold introduction
  \item \textbf{End-to-end sales execution} --- from list-building and cold
        outreach through discovery, demo, proposal, and close --- so internal
        leadership stays focused on delivery and product
  \item \textbf{RFP fluency} in regulated buyer verticals (medical, healthcare,
        education, government-adjacent) where Vivified's service profile is a
        strong fit but the response motion is the main gating factor
  \item \textbf{A vendor-neutral lens} on cloud platform selection, with
        hands-on experience evaluating interpretation and language-services
        delivery platforms against feature coverage, TCO, and roadmap fit
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 3 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Scope of Services}

\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}3.1 \quad LSP Partnership Outreach}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Build a target list of Language Service Providers whose language mix, vertical exposure, and service profile complement Vivified's
  \item Lead outreach into those LSPs, with Sahar personally leveraging prior relationships and industry contacts to short-circuit the introduction cycle
  \item Drive partnership conversations through structure (rate cards, SLAs, technology integration, co-selling motions) and bring them to a signed working relationship
  \item Maintain a partnership pipeline with stage, owner, next step, and status visible to Vivified leadership
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}3.2 \quad Direct Client Acquisition}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Build and continuously refine curated prospect lists in the priority verticals (medical, healthcare, education, and adjacent regulated buyers)
  \item Run targeted cold-calling and cold-emailing campaigns, with sequenced follow-up and disciplined response handling
  \item Own the sales cycle end-to-end --- from first touch through discovery, qualification, demo coordination, proposal, and close --- and document each opportunity in a shared pipeline
  \item Iterate on messaging, scripts, and cadences based on open / reply / meeting data, not guesswork
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}3.3 \quad Sales Collateral \& RFP Execution}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Create a Vivified-branded sales deck, capability statement, vertical one-pagers, case-study templates, and leave-behinds suitable for both outbound and finalist meetings
  \item Identify relevant RFPs, RFIs, and RFQs in target verticals and government / institutional channels, and bring a bid / no-bid recommendation to each
  \item Own the RFP lifecycle: intake, compliance matrix, written response, pricing input, internal review, and submission
  \item Prepare and rehearse finalist presentations, orals, and clarification responses
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{TPDark}3.4 \quad Cloud Platform Selection}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item Work with the Vivified team to define functional, technical, security, and budget requirements for the target cloud-based delivery platform
  \item Run a structured market scan and short-list of candidate platforms; compare across feature coverage, total cost of ownership, integration footprint, and roadmap fit
  \item Coordinate demos, reference calls, pilot scoping, and commercial negotiation
  \item Deliver a recommendation memo and decision support for final selection
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 5 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Investment \& Commercial Terms}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Monthly retainer:} \$4{,}500 USD per calendar month, flat
  \item \textbf{Time commitment:} approximately 10 hours per calendar week, scheduled flexibly
  \item \textbf{Billing:} monthly invoice on or near the first business day of each month; payment Net~15 via wire transfer, Zelle, Venmo, or PayPal
  \item \textbf{No commission, revenue share, or success fee} is payable; compensation is strictly the monthly retainer
  \item \textbf{Unused hours} do not roll forward; sustained increases beyond the weekly commitment are discussed and agreed in writing and may result in an adjusted retainer
\end{itemize}

%% ── Section 6 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Term, Termination \& Confidentiality}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt, topsep=4pt]
  \item Engagement runs on a \textbf{month-to-month} basis from the effective date, terminable by either party with \textbf{30 days written notice}
  \item Upon termination, Vivified pays Consultant the prorated portion of the monthly retainer through the effective termination date
  \item Both parties hold each other's confidential information (prospect lists, partner relationships, pricing, financials, strategy) in strict confidence, surviving termination for \textbf{three (3)~years}
  \item Work product specifically prepared for Vivified --- decks, RFP responses, prospect lists, and related materials --- belongs to Vivified upon payment, while Consultant retains pre-existing methodologies, templates, and frameworks
\end{itemize}
