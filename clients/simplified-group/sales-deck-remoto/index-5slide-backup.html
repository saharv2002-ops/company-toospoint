What we're looking for in a platform

question, answered.

The value flows
both ways.

The ecosystem Remoto described only works if agencies show up with real roster, real demand, and honest feedback. Here's what we bring — and what we'd want back from the network.

What Simplified brings to Remoto

A live agency, ready to feed the network

An early partner Remoto can actually pressure-test with — not a logo, an operator.

  • Vetted interpreters made available for Remoto assignments during idle time.
  • Real workflow data across OPI, VRI and multilingual customer service.
  • Regulated-vertical requirements (medical, education) as roadmap input.
  • Design-partner feedback on agency console, reporting, and rev-share mechanics.
  • A recurring book of business that stress-tests routing, QA and SLAs at volume.
What we'd want back from the network

Coverage, tooling & a shared roadmap

The reason we'd consolidate onto a partner platform instead of building — spelled out plainly.

  • Overflow coverage from Remoto's vetted pool for surge and rare-language demand.
  • An agency console that manages our clients, roster and billing without owning them.
  • Transparent economics on both directions — inbound assignments and idle-time work.
  • A clear, contractual line on data ownership, portability and non-poaching.
  • Early influence over the agency-partner roadmap in exchange for real usage.
