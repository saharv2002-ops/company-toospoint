# Semantix

Nordic-headquartered LSP (Stockholm), historically the dominant public-sector interpretation provider in Sweden and across the Nordics, with substantial translation and localization operations. Owned by Segulah private equity.

Why a good ToosPoint target: Semantix runs meaningful public-sector interpretation volume – healthcare, courts, immigration – which is exactly the operating profile ToosPoint speaks to fluently. Public-sector RFPs live and die on delivery credibility. Rare-language coverage is also a permanent challenge given Nordic refugee and immigration flows.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. Rare-language coverage expansion
3. RFP / public-sector win support
