**Subject:** Sourcing rare-language interpreters through community anchors

Hi there,

The sourcing playbook that consistently works for hard-to-staff languages is not job boards or recruiters – it is community-anchored: diaspora organizations, faith communities, university language departments, and a per-hour floor rather than pure per-minute pay so interpreters can predict their week. Nordic public-sector demand mixes very specific rare-language needs with unpredictable volume, which makes the retention side even harder than the sourcing side.

I run ToosPoint, and I sit inside Center Tel BPO week to week (1,000+ interpreters across 50+ languages). Rare-language sourcing and retention is one of the recurring problems I get pulled into.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare notes on which language cohorts are hardest for you right now? Happy to talk through specific sourcing patterns I have seen hold.
