# CyraCom

Arizona-headquartered interpretation specialist, historically one of the top three US OPI/VRI providers alongside LanguageLine and (now) AMN. Deep healthcare vertical, strong contact centers in Arizona and elsewhere, established government and 911 work.

Why a good ToosPoint target: CyraCom is a pure-play interpretation shop, which means every operating problem ToosPoint is built for lands directly on their P&L. Interpreter labor market pressure, healthcare QA, and vertical retention are all live concerns.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. QA and interpreter training programs
3. Client / vertical retention programs
