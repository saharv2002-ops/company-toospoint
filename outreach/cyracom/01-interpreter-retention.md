**Subject:** The leading indicator most OPI shops still do not track

Hi there,

Every OPI operation I have looked inside tracks interpreter attrition monthly, but almost none of them track the leading indicator that actually predicts it – the ratio of paid-idle minutes to paid-active minutes in the first 90 days. Interpreters do not leave first because of pay – they leave because the shift feels dead and they cannot forecast their week.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most operator time on each week.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare how your first-90-day journey looks today against the weekly metrics we run at Center Tel?
