The healthcare QA rubrics that catch problems earliest are weighted by session type rather than language or shift – because an interpreter can be strong in scheduled clinic and weak in ED trauma, and a language-first rubric will not surface it until a client complaint lands. Pair session-type weighting with a small calibration cell owning coaching escalations directly, and the score moves.
