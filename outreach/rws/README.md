# RWS Holdings

UK-headquartered, publicly listed until recently, one of the largest LSPs globally with deep roots in intellectual property translation and regulated industries (life sciences, patents, legal). Owns Trados (the dominant translator productivity suite) and has been pushing hard into AI-augmented language services with the Evolve and Language Weaver stacks. Acquired SDL in 2020 – still absorbing structurally.

Why a good ToosPoint target: RWS has huge translation depth but a comparatively smaller interpretation footprint; the operational tension is around blending AI-first translation workflows with the human review layer, and around post-SDL integration hangovers on the delivery side. Both are areas where a hands-on operator can move faster than internal transformation programs.

Angles being tried:
1. AI + human ops blending
2. Post-M&A operational integration (SDL residue)
3. QA and interpreter/reviewer training programs
