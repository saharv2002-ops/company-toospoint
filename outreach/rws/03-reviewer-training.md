**Subject:** Reviewer coaching at scale – what actually moves the score

Hi there,

At RWS-scale reviewer networks, the standard QA loop is score, feedback, repeat – and past a certain size that loop stops moving the quality needle because feedback is asynchronous and generic. The thing I have seen work at other LSPs is a small weekly calibration cell (3-5 senior reviewers) that owns coaching escalations rather than pushing them back to project managers.

I run ToosPoint, an operations consulting practice. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters across 50+ languages) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. QA calibration and coaching design is where I get pulled in most often.

Would 30 minutes be useful to compare notes? I would want to hear how your current reviewer feedback loop is structured, and I can walk through what the calibration cell model looks like in practice.
