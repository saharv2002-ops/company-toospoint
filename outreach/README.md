## About ToosPoint

ToosPoint is a hands-on operations consulting firm owned and led by Sahar Feiz. We work inside BPO, CX, and interpretation businesses on the operational problems that actually move revenue and retention – interpreter recruitment and coaching, QA and calibration, capacity planning, RFP support, post-M&A integration, and blending AI into human workflows without breaking them. We are currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ agents, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI), running growth and positioning work with iCall International, and consulting with Simplified Group. Engagements are typically a monthly retainer at roughly 15 hours per week. What makes Sahar different from the usual consultant deck is that she is in the operator seat every week – live P&Ls, live rosters, live client escalations.

## How to use these drafts

Each company folder contains a short snapshot (`README.md`) plus three angle-specific cold-email drafts. Drafts are written to be sent from Sahar directly to a suggested title (VP Operations, Head of Interpretation, Chief Delivery Officer, etc.). Before sending:

1. Confirm the recipient by name via LinkedIn – do not send to a generic title.
2. Verify anything you cite is still publicly true (leadership changes, acquisitions, product launches).
3. Trim further if the person is known to prefer short mail.
4. Never invent stats about the target or ToosPoint credentials beyond the three real client relationships.
