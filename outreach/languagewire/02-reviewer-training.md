**To / Suggested recipient title:** Director of Quality or Head of Reviewer Programs

**Subject:** Coaching loops that survive the AI shift

Hi there,

Reviewer coaching gets harder as the raw work shifts to MT, because the errors reviewers see are systematically different – fluent, plausible, and structurally biased. Traditional score-plus-feedback loops trained on human-drafted errors do not sharpen linguists against machine-drafted errors. The LSPs I have seen adapt fastest ran a small weekly calibration cell whose only job was to catalog the new error taxonomy and update coaching content in near real time.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. QA calibration design is where I get pulled in most often.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare notes on how your coaching loop is adapting?
