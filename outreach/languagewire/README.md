# LanguageWire

Copenhagen-headquartered enterprise LSP with a technology-forward positioning – Smart Editor, Smart MT, AI Translation. Strong across European enterprise accounts, particularly in Nordics, DACH and Benelux. Backed by CataCap and Nordic Capital.

Why a good ToosPoint target: LanguageWire's story is "technology plus linguists," which means the ops question is always where the human review layer sits, how it is priced, and how reviewers stay motivated when MT does more of the raw work. Interpretation is not their core, so this is a translation-side conversation focused on the AI+human blend and reviewer economics.

Angles being tried:
1. AI + human ops blending
2. QA and reviewer training programs
3. Nearshore delivery capacity augmentation
