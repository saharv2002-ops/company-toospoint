# LanguageLine Solutions

The category-defining OPI provider in the US, owned by Teleperformance since 2016. Massive interpreter network, deep healthcare book, strong government contracts, and a very established VRI product. Effectively the incumbent every other interpretation shop is measured against.

Why a good ToosPoint target: LanguageLine's operating challenge is not scale, it is the interpreter labor market – recruiting, onboarding, and retaining across the languages competitors are hunting them for. The Teleperformance ownership also means there is constant pressure to shave delivery cost per minute without touching client-facing quality. That is exactly the kind of tension a live-in-the-BPO consultant can help with.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. Nearshore delivery capacity augmentation (via Center Tel BPO)
3. Rare-language coverage expansion
