**Subject:** Rare-language coverage – the sourcing playbook that actually works

Hi there,

The unglamorous part of rare-language coverage is not finding the interpreters, it is retaining them once you have – because they get poached the moment a competitor has one client demanding that language. The playbook that has worked for the OPI operations I sit inside is community-anchored (diaspora networks, faith communities, university language departments) plus a per-hour guaranteed floor rather than pure per-minute pay.

I run ToosPoint, and I work week to week inside Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages) plus iCall International and Simplified Group. Rare-language sourcing and retention is one of the recurring problems I get pulled into.

Would 30 minutes be useful to compare notes on which language cohorts are hardest for you right now and where I have seen sourcing models actually hold?
