# Welocalize

Global LSP headquartered in Frederick, MD, backed by Norwest Equity Partners. Strong in enterprise localization, life sciences (regulated content), and AI data services (linguistic evaluation, model training data). Has grown through acquisition – Adapt Worldwide, Park IP, VIA, Global Language Solutions and others.

Why a good ToosPoint target: Welocalize sits at the intersection of traditional localization and the emerging AI-data-services market. That means operational tension between two very different delivery models running under the same roof. Post-acquisition integration is also an ongoing theme.

Angles being tried:
1. AI + human ops blending (LLM evaluation, data services workflows)
2. Post-M&A operational integration
3. QA and reviewer training programs
