# GLOBO Language Solutions

Philadelphia-headquartered interpretation and translation provider, heavily healthcare and enterprise-focused. Known for its technology platform (GLOBO HQ) and 200+ language coverage. Private-equity backed, growth-mode.

Why a good ToosPoint target: GLOBO is a technology-forward interpretation shop with growth momentum, which means the same operating tensions as other scaling OPI/VRI players – interpreter retention, QA discipline at pace, and rare-language coverage. The platform positioning also opens the door to conversations about how the human ops model complements the tech.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. Rare-language coverage expansion
3. QA and interpreter training programs
