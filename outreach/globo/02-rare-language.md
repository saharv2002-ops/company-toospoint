**Subject:** Rare-language coverage – sourcing that also retains

Hi there,

The 200-plus language coverage GLOBO is known for is genuinely hard to build, and even harder to retain. The playbook that has worked at the OPI shops I sit inside is community-anchored sourcing (diaspora, faith communities, university language departments) plus a per-hour guaranteed floor rather than per-minute pay, so rare-language interpreters can predict their week. Without that, you lose them to whichever competitor is prospecting them this month.

I run ToosPoint, and I sit inside Center Tel BPO week to week (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages). Rare-language sourcing and retention is one of the recurring problems I get pulled into.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare notes on which language cohorts are hardest for you right now and where I have seen sourcing models actually hold?
