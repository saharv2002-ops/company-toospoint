**Subject:** Retention leading indicators during a growth phase

Hi there,

Technology-forward interpretation shops usually assume the platform is the retention lever – better routing, better session experience, faster payouts. In practice, retention is decided in the first 90 days by two very unglamorous things: whether the interpreter's shift felt busy enough (idle-to-active minute ratio) and whether there is a named human they can escalate to when a session goes sideways.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most operator time on each week.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare how your first-90-day journey is designed today against what I have seen hold at other shops?
