**Subject:** Healthcare QA – session type matters more than language

Hi there,

Healthcare interpretation QA that samples by language and shift misses the pattern that matters – session type. An interpreter can be strong across scheduled clinic visits and weak in ED trauma, and a language-and-shift rubric will not surface it until the client complaint lands. The version of QA that catches this earlier is session-type-weighted sampling plus a small calibration cell owning coaching.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. QA and coaching design is where I get pulled in most often.
