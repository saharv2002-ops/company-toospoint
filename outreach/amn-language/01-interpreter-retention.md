Healthcare interpreters attrit for reasons that generic workforce dashboards miss. It is not shift length or pay first – it is the emotional load of specific session types (ED trauma, oncology, end-of-life) and the absence of a same-day debrief path. Parent-company workforce muscle is usually built for nurses or allied health, and it treats interpreters like a staffing category rather than a subculture with its own retention mechanics.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most operator time on.
