# AMN Language Services

Interpretation division of AMN Healthcare, one of the largest US healthcare workforce companies. Built through the acquisition of Stratus Video and MedCall Advisors. Deep healthcare vertical – VRI, OPI, and on-site interpretation across US hospital systems.

Why a good ToosPoint target: AMN's parent is a workforce company, so operational discipline is already high, but the interpretation business is a specific subculture that does not always fit the parent's staffing muscle. Interpreter retention, healthcare-specific QA, and the constant pressure to defend margins on high-volume VRI contracts are the enduring problems.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. QA and interpreter training programs (healthcare-specific)
3. Nearshore delivery capacity augmentation
