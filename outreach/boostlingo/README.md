# Boostlingo

Nashville-headquartered interpretation technology platform (not an LSP competitor). Powers on-demand OPI/VRI for hundreds of LSPs, hospitals, and enterprises. Ecosystem includes Boostlingo Professional Interpreter Network (BPIN), scheduling, and AI-assisted interpretation tooling.

Why a good ToosPoint target: This is a partnership pitch, not a consulting pitch. ToosPoint's client Center Tel BPO – a 1,000+ agent OPI/VRI shop – is precisely the kind of delivery capacity that Boostlingo's LSP customers periodically need to scale into. iCall International also operates on the Boostlingo platform. There is a two-way partnership conversation to be had: Center Tel as a preferred capacity source for Boostlingo customers, and ToosPoint as a delivery / ops partner to LSPs that grow on Boostlingo but need help operationalizing.

Angles being tried:
1. Platform partnership / channel – Center Tel BPO as vetted capacity source for Boostlingo LSP customers
2. ToosPoint as ops-consulting partner for Boostlingo-native LSPs growing past their operational muscle
3. Interpreter recruitment and retention insight – Boostlingo sees the meta-data, ToosPoint runs the shifts
