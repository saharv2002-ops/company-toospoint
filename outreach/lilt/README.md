# LILT

San Francisco-headquartered adaptive machine translation company that has positioned itself between traditional LSPs and pure MT vendors. Strong federal / defense footprint (US Air Force, USSOCOM, and other agencies), enterprise translation platform, and a growing services layer around its Contextual AI Engine.

Why a good ToosPoint target: LILT's ops question is the classic AI-plus-human tension: their linguist network has to review MT output at scale, and their federal contracts demand delivery credibility that pure-tech vendors cannot fake. Vertical growth (defense, healthcare, financial services) creates recurring RFP work, and rapid growth phases invite integration debt.

Angles being tried:
1. AI + human ops blending
2. RFP / vertical win support (federal + regulated)
3. QA and reviewer training programs
