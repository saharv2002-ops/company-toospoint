**Subject:** Interpreter attrition – the piece most LSPs measure wrong

Hi there,

Every OPI and VRI operation I have looked inside measures interpreter attrition monthly, but almost none of them measure the leading indicator that actually predicts it – the ratio of paid-idle minutes to paid-active minutes in the first 90 days. Interpreters do not leave because of pay first; they leave because the shift feels dead and they cannot forecast their week.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO, an OPI/VRI shop with 1,000+ interpreters across 50+ languages, and I do growth and operations work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Retention is the problem I spend the most time on across all three.

For a group the size of TransPerfect's interpretation division, I would love to compare notes on what your first-90-day interpreter journey looks like today, and where I usually see one or two structural changes free up several points of retention.
