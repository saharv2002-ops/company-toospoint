# TransPerfect

Largest privately held LSP in the world, headquartered in New York, with reported revenue over one billion dollars and operations across 100+ cities. Broad service portfolio: translation, interpretation (OPI/VRI), localization, e-discovery, life sciences, and a growing tech stack (GlobalLink). Aggressive acquirer – regularly integrates smaller LSPs and vertical specialists.

Why a good ToosPoint target: at TransPerfect's scale, the operational drag comes from post-acquisition interpreter roster consolidation, QA calibration across dozens of business units, and vertical-specific delivery capacity (healthcare, legal, government). An operator-led consultant who can plug in on one workflow at a time is easier to buy than another big-four engagement.

Angles being tried:
1. Post-M&A operational integration
2. Interpreter recruitment and retention (OPI/VRI side)
3. QA and interpreter training programs
