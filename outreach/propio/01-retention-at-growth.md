**Subject:** Retention curves during a growth phase break differently

Hi there,

The retention problem for fast-growing OPI/VRI shops is not the same as the retention problem for mature ones. When intake volume is high, first-90-day churn hides inside gross-add numbers and the leading indicators – idle-to-active minute ratio, escalation response time, named-manager-to-interpreter ratio – get diluted. By the time monthly attrition moves, you have already lost several cohorts.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most operator time on each week.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare how your first-90-day interpreter metrics are structured during growth? Happy to walk through the specific weekly cuts we run.
