# Propio Language Services

Kansas-headquartered interpretation provider, one of the fastest-growing OPI/VRI shops in the US over the last several years. Deep healthcare, government, and education verticals. Private-equity backed.

Why a good ToosPoint target: Fast-growing PE-backed interpretation shops hit predictable operating walls – recruiting can outrun training, QA falls behind volume, and vertical account teams start firefighting. A hands-on operator can help stabilize the growth curve without slowing it down.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention
2. QA and interpreter training programs
3. Nearshore delivery capacity augmentation
