# Cloudbreak / Voyce

Cloudbreak Health is a video-first healthcare interpretation platform, historically a Martti device-and-service provider deeply embedded in US hospital systems. Voyce is a growing video interpretation platform focused on healthcare, hospitality, and enterprise. Both operate at the intersection of device/platform delivery and human interpreter delivery.

Why a good ToosPoint target: Video-first interpretation shops carry the same interpreter workforce challenges as traditional OPI providers, with added pressure from device-uptime SLAs and hospital IT integration. Growth ambitions in adjacent verticals (hospitality, education, financial services) also create RFP surface area.

Angles being tried:
1. Interpreter recruitment and retention (video-specific)
2. QA and interpreter training programs
3. New market entry (adjacent verticals beyond healthcare)
