**Subject:** VRI-specific retention – camera fatigue is real

Hi there,

Video interpretation retention curves diverge from voice-only OPI in a specific way: camera fatigue and the emotional weight of visible patient reactions push a distinct cohort out around month 4-6. It is not the first-90-day cliff – it is a second, later cliff that most workforce dashboards do not surface because they only alarm on 90-day metrics.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most operator time on each week.
