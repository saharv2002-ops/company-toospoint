**Subject:** Session-type-weighted QA for video interpretation

Hi there,

VRI QA rubrics that sample by language and shift miss the pattern that actually matters – session type. A video interpreter can be strong across scheduled clinic visits and weak in ED trauma or bedside end-of-life, and a language-and-shift rubric will not surface it until a hospital client complaint lands. Session-type-weighted sampling plus a small calibration cell owning coaching escalations catches it earlier.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, 50+ languages, OPI and VRI) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. QA and coaching design is where I get pulled in most often.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to compare how your current sampling framework is structured?
