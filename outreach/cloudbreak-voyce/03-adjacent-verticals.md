**Subject:** Adjacent verticals – what breaks when the healthcare playbook travels

Hi there,

Video-first interpretation shops moving from healthcare into adjacent verticals – hospitality, education, financial services – usually assume the delivery playbook travels. It does not. Session length distributions are different, escalation expectations are different, and the client success motion looks nothing like a hospital account. The most common failure is trying to run adjacent verticals on the healthcare delivery org rather than building a small parallel pod.

I run ToosPoint. I sit inside Center Tel BPO week to week and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. New-vertical entry ops is a piece I have run a few times.

Would a 30-minute call be useful to talk about which adjacent vertical is on the roadmap and where the operations model needs to look different?
