Translation-first LSPs that scale up interpretation as a second business typically hit the same wall at the same time: the recruiting engine can find interpreters, but the ops model – built for translation projects – cannot hold them past 90 days. Interpreters need same-day escalation paths and predictable shift shapes, not project managers.

I run ToosPoint. I am currently retained by Center Tel BPO (1,000+ interpreters, OPI and VRI, 50+ languages) and work with iCall International and Simplified Group. Interpreter retention is the workstream I spend the most time on.

If Lionbridge is investing in growing interpretation, I would love to compare notes on where the ops model needs to look different from the translation delivery muscle. A 30-minute call, no deck.
