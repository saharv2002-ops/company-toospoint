# Lionbridge

One of the largest global LSPs, headquartered in Waltham, MA. Broad translation and localization portfolio, historically strong in games, life sciences, and enterprise localization. Divested its AI data services business (Lionbridge AI) to TELUS International in 2020, refocusing on core language services. Owned by private equity (H.I.G. Capital).

Why a good ToosPoint target: PE ownership means constant margin pressure, and Lionbridge's post-divestiture focus is on making the core translation and interpretation delivery leaner. Vertical wins (life sciences, legal, gaming) depend on RFP execution and delivery credibility; interpretation is a growth area rather than the core.

Angles being tried:
1. RFP / vertical win support
2. Interpreter recruitment and retention
3. Nearshore delivery capacity augmentation
