# Acolad Group

Paris-headquartered European LSP giant, formed and grown through the roll-up of Technicis, Livewords, Amplexor, TextMaster and others. Large multilingual delivery network across Europe, growing footprint in North America, strong in life sciences and industrial verticals.

Why a good ToosPoint target: Acolad's identity is a serial roll-up, which means integration workstreams are effectively continuous. European labor market for interpreters and reviewers is structurally different from the US, but the retention and calibration mechanics are the same. Nearshore capacity conversations also travel well across the Atlantic.

Angles being tried:
1. Post-M&A operational integration
2. QA and reviewer training programs
3. New market entry (specifically North American interpretation growth)
